## Product promise

RUNQ is a professional workspace for creating, preparing, rehearsing and operating scripted shows. It combines conventional cue-editing and audio-production tools with an AI assistant that understands the show and can help prepare an entire cue workflow in a few clicks. Operators review proposals and retain control of playback.

**Primary English pitch:**

> RUNQ is an intelligent show-production workstation. Go from a theatre script to a prepared, reviewable show in just a few clicks — with contextual AI, professional sound-cue tools, intelligent rehearsal and deterministic live control.

**Spanish pitch:**

> RUNQ es una estación de trabajo inteligente para la producción de espectáculos. Convierte un guion en un espectáculo preparado y revisable en pocos clics, con un asistente de IA especializado, herramientas profesionales de audio, ensayo inteligente y control directo del operador.

## Product pillars

1. **Professional workstation:** Cue lists and editor, waveform-based audio work, layered audio, fades, loops, stops and show transport.
2. **RUNQ AI:** A specialized assistant aware of the current script, cues, assets, show state and application workflows. It proposes and explains actions for human review.
3. **Whole-show preparation:** From script import to cue suggestions, sound preparation and a reviewable show in a few clicks. Avoid claiming unattended finalization or guaranteed complete artistic correctness.
4. **Intelligent rehearsal:** Voice cue recognition and rehearsal autopilot where implemented; distinguish rehearsal automation from live show control.
5. **Deterministic live operation:** Explicit GO/STOP and operator control, not AI-triggered performance.
6. **Portable show workflow:** Preserve script-to-cue traceability and export full-show ZIP with media.

## Competitive narrative

Traditional cueing systems emphasize cue playback and detailed operator control. RUNQ's intended differentiator is continuity: **script → AI-assisted preparation → sound assets → cue editing → rehearsal → live operation**, without losing operator oversight. Avoid unsupported claims of feature parity or superiority to QLab, Show Cue System or other products.
