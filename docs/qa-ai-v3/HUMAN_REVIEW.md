# Independent human review still required

Automation can verify exact source references, present cue/asset IDs, current
project isolation, valid unapplied operation contracts and catalogue-backed
capability statements. Offline success does not rate Muse understanding.

A theatre operator should independently review:

- Whether functional advice is sufficient for a novice and useful to an expert.
- Artistic appropriateness of cue ideas without changing explicit script actions.
- The meaning of relative timing, overlapping sound layers and ambiguities.
- Whether explanations of pending/applied/revoked decisions remain understandable.
- Conversations switching Spanish/English and terminology across 50 turns.
- Whether an alternative after rejection actually respects the rejected idea.
- Whether the assistant clearly distinguishes evidence, uncertainty and hypothesis.

Holdout families must not be used to adjust prompts. A deterministic keyword,
status or target check is only its stated rubric, not a comprehensive semantic
judgment. Mark absent independent artistic/semantic ratings `NOT_RATED`.

Physical audio, microphone and hardware quality are outside this AI-only
campaign. No result here certifies readiness for a live performance.
