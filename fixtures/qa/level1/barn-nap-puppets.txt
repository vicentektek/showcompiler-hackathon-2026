SHOW 4 — The Barn Nap
(CUE 5: play the recorded rooster fanfare as the puppets wake up.)
(HEN PUPPET trots onto the tiny stage; the puppeteer's sneakers squeak for real on the boards.)
(CUE 5: fade the recorded rooster out at the first huge puppet yawn.)
PUPPET OWL: I napped to that rooster in my dreams, twice as proud.
(CUE 6: DEAD SILENCE FOR THE NAP, held by the operator's own hand after review; no automatic mute of the hall.)
(The children of the room hold their breath out loud; no speaker carries that.)
(CUE 7: play the recorded morning bell softly until the puppets dance.)
(APPLAUSE: the grown-ups clap in the room; the hall does the clapping here.)
(END OF SHOW: no file plays at the barn door; the room keeps its own noise.)
