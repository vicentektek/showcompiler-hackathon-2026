NIGHT CROSSING
An original two-person scene. Characters: ADA and FINN.
SCENE 1 — PLATFORM 9
[Recorded train-station ventilation starts as a low, seamless loop.]
ADA: Have you checked the last departure?
FINN: The timetable says 02:17.
ADA: Yesterday someone told me the siren had sounded, but that was a story.
[The existing station ventilation continues unchanged.]
[A recorded departure chime rings twice, once at each end of the platform.]
FINN: That's our train.
(FINN folds a real paper ticket; the rustle comes from the actor.)
[The recorded ventilation fades to a stop over three seconds.]
ADA: Listen to how quiet the station becomes.
[Complete silence for one beat. Do not play a silence track.]
FINN: Ready when you are.
[Blackout only. No sound cue.]
END
